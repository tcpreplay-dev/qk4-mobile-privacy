# QK4 Mobile Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-27

QK4 Mobile is a remote control app for Elecraft K4 transceivers. This policy describes what the iOS app does with your information.

The app is published on the App Store by Fred Klassen (the "developer"). It is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later, and the source code is available at https://github.com/worldwidedx/QK4-Mobile.

## Summary

- The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data from the app.
- The app has no user accounts.
- The app does not include advertising, analytics or tracking.
- Your radio connection and operating data stay between your device and your radio.

## Data the app handles on your device

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Radio address, name and password | Connect to your K4 | Stored on your device. Sent only to your radio. |
| Operating settings and preferences | Remember your layout, macros and options | Stored on your device only. |
| Logbook entries | Keep your contact log | Stored on your device only, unless you choose to upload to QRZ (see below). |
| QRZ.com credentials or API key (optional) | QRZ lookup and logbook upload | Stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Sent only to QRZ.com. |
| Audio from the microphone | Transmit audio (TX) to your radio | Streamed to your radio while you transmit. Not recorded or kept by the app. |

## Permissions

- **Local Network:** to find and connect to your K4 on your network.
- **Microphone:** to send your voice to the radio when you transmit. The app does not listen when you are not transmitting.
- **Bluetooth:** to pair an optional CTR2-MIDI controller over Bluetooth LE MIDI.
- **Background audio:** to keep receive audio playing when the app is in the background.
- **Camera:** not used. A usage description is declared only because a bundled media framework links camera APIs.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## Third-party services

If you enable the optional QRZ.com features, the app sends the callsigns you look up, and the log entries you upload, to QRZ.com using your own QRZ credentials. QRZ.com's own privacy policy applies to that data. The app does not contact any other third-party service.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Your choices

Because the developer does not receive your data, there is nothing for the developer to access or delete. You can remove all data the app stores on your device by deleting the app. You can remove a saved QRZ key within the app's settings, or by deleting the app.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
